# Nomadic Paws Photography Shot List

This is the source of truth for replacing temporary website imagery. Keep Cheeto's real appearance, every product's real color and design, and the current page layout. Do not add text to final photographs. Photograph the exact owned product whenever a product is named.

## Visual direction

- Warm, honest, outdoors-first photography; natural desert colors rather than heavy filters.
- Prefer open shade or golden hour. Protect highlight detail in orange fur and bright packaging.
- Keep the subject clear at phone size. Leave intentional negative space; do not fill every edge.
- Capture a clean landscape master plus a little extra space around the edges for responsive crops.
- Final delivery: full-resolution original plus an edited sRGB JPEG. Do not overwrite originals.

## Supporting captures for each product setup

For every priority setup, also take:

1. One clean product-only frame.
2. One Cheeto-looking-at-camera frame.
3. One real interaction frame.
4. One wide frame with generous negative space.
5. One vertical 4:5 frame for social posts.
6. A 5–10 second steady vertical video clip when the interaction is safe and natural.

## Desert reference library

- `images/reference/desert-mountain-clouds.jpg`: bright mountain/cloud reference with strong desert identity. Best lighting/composition reference for daytime product sessions; crop away the visible pipe and distant buildings.
- `images/reference/desert-sunset-silhouette.jpg`: wide warm sky reference with excellent negative space. Best color/mood reference for golden-hour or dusk sessions.
- The downloaded stitched panorama was rejected because seams and side bars are visible.
- The blue-hour residential views are useful location scouts, but buildings make them weaker as final product backgrounds.

## Asset and permission notes

- Cheeto and owned product photos are the preferred permanent assets.
- Pezzy package images currently used on the store are exact third-party product images. Keep proof of permission with the brand records; replace them with owned Cheeto/product photography when the final hero shots are ready unless the permission explicitly covers continued website use.
- Do not use generated cats, generated product packages, or altered labels as permanent commercial photography.
- Published Trail Journal cover art, logos, product gallery images, page copy, links, inventory behavior, checkout behavior, and SEO are outside this photography replacement pass.

## Replacement checklist

When a final photo is ready:

- Confirm the product, color, and model match the card.
- Confirm Cheeto's face, fur, markings, and eye color are unaltered.
- Export at least 1600 px wide in sRGB.
- Check the desktop crop and a 390 px mobile viewport.
- Replace only the temporary asset for that slot; do not rewrite the surrounding card.
- Keep descriptive alt text factual and concise.
