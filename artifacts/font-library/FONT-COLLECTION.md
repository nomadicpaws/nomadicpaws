# Nomadic Paws Font Collection

## Launch collection — eight families

1. **Cheeto Kitty** — the character font. Curly-cute forms with optional Cheeto face, ears, paws, whiskers, and striped-tail alternates.
2. **Desert Doodle** — playful curly hand lettering for lighthearted posts and warm personal moments.
3. **Campfire Glow** — rounded light-up-sign lettering for reels, announcements, and evening stories.
4. **Trail Journal** — an organic editorial serif for blog stories, quotations, and reflective captions.
5. **Roadtrip Marker** — energetic hand-lettered marker forms for travel notes and casual titles.
6. **Ranger Stamp** — sturdy condensed outdoor lettering for strong hooks, labels, and trail tips.
7. **Sunset Postcard** — cheerful retro travel lettering for destinations, memories, and promotional posts.
8. **Quiet Morning** — calm handwritten print for gentle updates, devotional thoughts, and intimate captions.

## Color behavior

- Main letter shapes are recolorable.
- Outline, shadow, and glow colors can be changed independently when the treatment supports them.
- Cheeto-specific illustrated accents keep their signature orange and darker tabby stripes by default.
- Studio should expose a default-on **Keep Cheeto colors** control when Cheeto Kitty alternates are used.
- Turning that control off may allow full recoloring, but it must never be required for ordinary use.

## Future expansion

The launch library intentionally contains eight families. Two additional positions remain open for a ten-family collection after the first eight have been tested in real posts and videos.
